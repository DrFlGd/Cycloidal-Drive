How the generator works

      
    
        
  1. Configure. Pick the preset closest to your motor and ratio, then change settings section by section. Settings that the current build does not use are hidden, and changed values are marked with a dot; the ↺ button returns one to the preset.
    2. 
        
  2. Preview. The real OpenSCAD design is compiled in your browser. Pick the assembly, an exploded view or any single part. Section cuts the model in half.
    3. 
        
  3. Check & export. Every change runs the design's own rule checks. When they pass, download single parts, a machining pack (DXF) or a 3D printing pack (STL), plus the specs and hardware list.
    4. 
      

      
Saved parameter sets open in the desktop OpenSCAD Customizer as well. Nothing leaves your computer: rendering happens locally.

      
This is a geometric and assembly-clearance design aid, not a load rating. Check bearing life, contact stress, fits and safe torque for your application.
