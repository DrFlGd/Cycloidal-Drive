# v1.6 release notes

v1.6 adds cumulative simplification levels while preserving the full bearing-supported pancake design as Level 0.

- Level 1 replaces outer ring roller hardware with printed case ridges.
- Level 2 also removes eccentric and output-pin roller bearings for direct sliding contact.
- Level 3 also removes the large output bearing and uses printed plain input/output journals.
- Animation, profiles, catalog hardware selection, manufacturing reports, and separated 3D layout remain available.
- 10 named profiles × 4 simplification levels were compiled against the current geometry assertions (40/40 pass).

Levels 2–3 are intended primarily for demonstrators and low-speed/light-duty prototypes; sliding printed interfaces increase friction, wear, heat and backlash.
